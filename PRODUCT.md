## Platform

web

## Users

Primary (confirmed 2026-11-14): club timekeepers. Whoever runs the clock and bell at club chukkas and matches, often a volunteer, groom or club manager standing at the pitch side, outdoors, frequently one-handed and in direct sunlight.

Other audiences: players organising their own practice chukkas; umpires; spectators and families following a match on the live board from the sideline or from home.

## Product Purpose

ChukkaTimer times polo chukkas and runs a polo match from a phone: countdown with the 30-second warning bell and end-of-chukka bell, extra time, score, handicap goals, and chukka-by-chukka history. Success is a timekeeper who can run a session without a separate stopwatch, bell and score sheet, and spectators who can follow without asking.

## Positioning

A timer that knows polo's rules rather than a general stopwatch: 7-minute chukkas, the 30-second bell, extra time (never on the last chukka), automatic HPA handicap goals, club-chukka rotations, and a live board any browser can open from a link or QR code. Made by one person.

## Operating Context

- This repository is the marketing site at chukkatimer.com: one static `index.html` with inline CSS, no build step, served by GitHub Pages (see `CNAME`). Its job is to explain the app and send people to the App Store and the live board.
- The other two surfaces live in the private app repository: the iOS app (SwiftUI; Simple Timer, Game Mode, Club Chukkas; Live Activities) and the live board at live.chukkatimer.com.
- Logo files in `assets/` are copied from the app repository, where `scripts/appicon/render.py` generates them. Copy the outputs; do not redraw the mark here.
- Pitch-side use: glanceable from arm's length, bells audible over a field, works offline.
- Rules source: HPA 2026 rule book (in the app repository).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Chukka length 1–15 minutes, extra time 0–60 seconds, choice of three bell sounds.
- Game Mode: team names, colours, handicaps, scores per chukka.
- Club Chukkas: rotating sides, "now and up next", find-me, from a chukka sheet.
- Live sharing by link and QR code; no app needed to view.
- No account required; works offline; App Store privacy label is "Data not collected".
- Web surfaces have no build step and no JS framework today.
- App needs Dynamic Type, dark mode, and legible timer numerals (tabular figures).
- Terminology: UI copy says "players", never "riders". A polo mallet is about 5.5:1 shaft to head.
- The App Store listing (checked 2026-11-14) is free and requires iOS 18.0 or later. Check the listing before stating requirements.

## Brand Commitments

Confirmed 2026-11-14, after two rounds of alternative directions:

- Visual world: "Clubhouse", the polo standard played straight: warm paper, enamel green, aged brass, a serif for display. This is a standing preference, chosen over ten alternatives.
- Quality bar: Wimbledon and The Open. Heritage sport done with restraint; premium without being flashy.
- Name: "ChukkaTimer", one word, everywhere (the app title currently shows two words and changes to match).
- Logo (chosen 2026-11-14): "the swing". One flat stroke that starts as a hairline, gathers weight as it travels round, and closes on a brass ball at twelve o'clock. The same ball dots the "i" of the wordmark. Two flat colours, no shading. Generated by `scripts/appicon/render.py` (`make appicon`). Sixteen rendered brass-and-enamel marks were rejected first as too complicated and too literal.

Owner's taste, as it turned out for the logo: simple and not literal. Rendered brass objects (stopwatch, dial, bell, shield) were all judged too complicated or too obvious; an earlier note that flat marks read as childlike applied to illustrative icon art, not to this mark.

## Product Principles

1. The clock is the product: time remaining is always the most legible thing on any surface.
2. Polo-correct before clever: behaviour follows the rule book and club practice.
3. Works with nothing: no account, no signal, no setup beyond a tap.
4. One person at the pitch side can run everything with one hand.
5. Spectators get the same truth as the timekeeper, live.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Outdoor sunlight legibility is a product requirement, so text and control contrast should meet WCAG AA at minimum; state must not be carried by colour alone.
