Get the app

A polo timer for iPhone.

ChukkaTimer times chukkas and runs a match from your iPhone: the 30-second bell, the end bell, extra time, the score and handicap goals. Spectators follow on a live board in any browser.

Download on theApp Store Open the live board

Free. Requires iOS 18 or later. No account needed.

Timer

The bell rings with 30 seconds to go and again at the end of the chukka, then extra time runs on. Start, pause and reset are each one tap, and the digits are large enough to read at arm's length.

Game mode

Each game holds its teams, the number of chukkas and the score, chukka by chukka. Games are saved on your phone, so you can stop and pick one up where you left off.

Handicap goals

Name each team, pick its colours and enter its handicap. ChukkaTimer works out the goals received under HPA rules and includes them in the score.

Club chukkas

Club chukkas don't fit a two-team fixture. Sides are picked chukka by chukka from whoever is playing that day. ChukkaTimer runs the session and puts up a live board that everyone at the club can open on their own phone.

  • Sides per chukka

    Set who plays for Whites and Blacks in each chukka. The line-up can change every time.

  • Now and up next

    The board shows who is on the pitch and who plays next, so the next players can start tacking up.

  • Find me

    Pick your name and the board marks your chukkas as on now, up next or done.

  • From the chukka sheet

    Laid out like the sheet clubs already use: players down the side, chukkas across the top, W or B in each cell.

Live board

Tap share for a link and a QR code. Spectators open it in any browser and see the score, the handicap goals and the clock as they change. They don't need the app or an account.

Details

Lock Screen
A Live Activity keeps the countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you can check the time without unlocking the phone.
Bells
Three bell sounds to choose from.
Chukka length
Seven minutes as standard. Change the length and the extra time to suit your club.
No signal
The timer and game mode run on your phone and work offline. Only live sharing uses the network.
No account
Open the app and start timing. There is nothing to sign up for.
Privacy
The App Store privacy label for ChukkaTimer is “Data not collected”.
Price
Free on the App Store. Requires iOS 18 or later.
